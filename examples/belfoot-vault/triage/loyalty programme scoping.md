Forwarded by Thomas after the commercial team's offsite. Their working draft, not ours.

The idea: a points-based loyalty scheme on the season-ticket chip. Points for attendance, for spend at concessions, for buying merchandise, redeemable against upgrades and club-shop credit. They have costed a vendor at roughly EUR 40k for year one and want a decision before the season opener.

Two things in it touch our programme:

- The points balance would have to live somewhere, and they are assuming the cashless wallet holds it. Nobody has asked whether that is true. The wallet is a closed-loop payment instrument, and stapling a loyalty ledger onto it changes what it is, including for the regulator conversation on AML reporting.
- Redemption at concessions would run through the same terminals we are installing, in the same window as the soft launch.

Thomas is not asking me to run this. He is asking whether it can ride on what we are building.

My read: the answer is probably yes technically and no this season. But that is a scoping conversation for the club, not a task for this engagement. The loyalty programme sits outside the engagement letter and has its own workstream.
