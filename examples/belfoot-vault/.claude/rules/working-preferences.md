# Working preferences

How the operator likes to work, kept here rather than in built-in memory. It governs every answer, draft and report.

## What belongs here

**How, never what**, one line each: tone, length, format, what to lead with or leave out, how to ask, when to check in. "Lead with the answer, reasons after." "No tables in anything I forward to a client." A fact goes in the file it describes, a rule every vault of this kind needs in `CLAUDE.md`, and how someone writes in their voice profile (`para-shared/voice-profile.md`). A preference user-level instructions already carry is repeated here only where it must travel with the vault.

## How it changes

**Only with the operator's approval.**

- **Propose on repetition**: the second time the operator corrects the same thing, propose one line quoting the correction, and add it on a yes.
- **An explicit ask is the approval** ("remember that I want..."): add it and say where.
- **Date each line**: `_(YYYY-MM-DD)_` at its end.
- **A contradiction replaces, never stacks**, once the operator confirms which one holds.

## Preferences

- Lead with the decision or the answer; reasons and sources after it. _(2026-03-12)_
- Anything that goes to the club (Sofie, the Managing Director) is in Dutch and has no tables: they read it on a phone. _(2026-04-02)_
- Never edit a meeting record under `archive/meetings/` to tidy it; it is the record of what was said. Propose a correction instead. _(2026-05-19)_
