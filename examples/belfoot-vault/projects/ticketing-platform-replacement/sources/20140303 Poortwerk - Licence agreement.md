# Licence agreement - Poortwerk ticketing platform

**Licensor:** Poortwerk Ticketing NV
**Licensee:** BelFoot Royal Sporting Club
**Signed:** 2014-03-03
**Reference:** PW-LIC-2014-118

## Term

The licence runs from 2014-03-03 to 2014-12-31 and renews automatically for one calendar year on each 1 January, unless either party gives notice by registered letter at least three months before the renewal date.

## Fees

Annual licence fee: €96,000, invoiced each January.

## Support

Second-line support during office hours and on match days. Support for a platform version ends on the date Poortwerk announces in writing.
