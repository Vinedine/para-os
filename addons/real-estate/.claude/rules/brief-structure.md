# Brief structure

**Order:** fixed for a property, not fixed for any other brief.

## The shape

**A property's brief follows [property-dossier.md](property-dossier.md)**, which takes over every brief its paths name: a prospect in `resources/ideas/`, a deal in `projects/`, a sold property in `archive/properties/`, a dropped one in `archive/researched-deals/`. The section order is there, not here.

Every other brief (a non-property project, idea or area) is shaped by what it documents and keeps the conventions [readme-structure.md](readme-structure.md) states for every non-property document.

As in [property-dossier.md](property-dossier.md#placeholders).
