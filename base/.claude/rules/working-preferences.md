# Working preferences

How the operator likes to work, kept here rather than in built-in memory. It governs every answer, draft and report.

## What belongs here

**How, never what**, one line each: tone, length, format, what to lead with or leave out, how to ask, when to check in. "Lead with the answer, reasons after." "No tables in anything I forward to a client." A rule every vault of this kind needs goes in `CLAUDE.md`, and how someone writes in their voice profile (`para-shared/voice-profile.md`). A preference user-level instructions already carry is repeated here only where it must travel with the vault.

## How it changes

**Only with the operator's approval.**

- **An explicit ask is the approval** ("remember that I want..."): add it and say where.
- **Date each line**: `_(YYYY-MM-DD)_` at its end.
- **A contradiction replaces, never stacks**, once the operator confirms which one holds.
