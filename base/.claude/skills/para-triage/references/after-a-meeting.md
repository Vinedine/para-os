# After a meeting record is filed

A dated conversation record (a call note, minutes, a recorder's summary or transcript) filed for an entity whose shape declares `Last touch` carries the follow-on set below, beside [approval.md](approval.md#the-manifest)'s README and brief edits. Each one is listed under the manifest, or under the table, and applies only if its item is approved; [execute.md](execute.md#follow-ons-step-8) writes them.

**The shape decides which fields exist**: the entity's rule file, or the lifecycle section standing for one ([operating-discipline.md](../../para-shared/operating-discipline.md#a-vaults-rule-files)). A field it does not declare is never proposed. Read the entity's header or register row, and the card its header links as its contact (a deal's `Champion`), before proposing.

**Everything comes from the record**: its own date, never the file's or the run's, and what was said, never what the meeting's title suggests. A record holding only a title, an invite or an empty summary proposes Last touch at most, and no draft. The record's own checkboxes stay [frozen](../../para-shared/operating-discipline.md#leave-other-peoples-words-alone).

1. **Last touch**: `<the record's date>, <one clause on what happened>`, shown as `<current> → <proposed>`. Only where the record's date is later than the current one, or the current one is `unknown`.
2. **Signer**: where the record names who commits the money, shown the same way, linked to their card where one exists. Nothing where the current value already names that person.
3. **One next step**: our earliest commitment in the record, worded as **Add action** words it ([approval.md](approval.md#the-action-vocabulary)). Its home is the contact's card, under its next-actions heading, as far as the scan's `contact_card_level` allows (at `relationship only`, a reply, introduction, thanks or check-in only); otherwise the entity's own: a register row's next-step cell, shown as current then proposed; for an entity under `resources/`, an open item in the owning area's `actions.md` linking its brief; for a project, its `actions.md`. **Their earliest commitment** is one `Waiting on [<person>](<card>): <what> (since <the record's date>)` line, with no `📅`, homed the same way; the rest go in the draft. An open item the record shows done is proposed ticked, any other stays as the operator wrote it, and a line already there is never added twice.
4. **A stage question**, never a stage edit, and only where the record shows the current stage's exit criterion met (the lifecycle table's column): `Move <entity> on from <stage>? Its exit criterion, <criterion>, looks met: <evidence>.` The summary repeats it. Whatever the answer, triage writes no Stage line ([lifecycles.md](../../para-shared/lifecycles.md#what-each-skill-does-with-one)).
5. **A follow-up draft**: the agreed steps, our promises with their dates, and the next meeting, per [drafting.md](../../para-shared/drafting.md), which says where its recipients come from. Shown in full, never sent or saved to a mailbox.
