# Drafting for the operator (shared across skills)

Read before drafting anything the operator will paste and send: a mail, a message, a letter.

1. **Name what it answers** above the draft: the thread and message by sender and date, or the meeting record by its date.
2. **Real recipients.** To and CC are addresses from the thread, the meeting's calendar invite or the contact card in `areas/network/`, never a bare name, a placeholder, a group or an address only a transcript mentions. Look an unknown one up in each before asking.
3. **Nothing the vault does not hold.** A price, date or commitment neither the thread nor the vault contains is marked inline: `[date: not in the vault]`.
4. **A complaint gets an acknowledgement**, not a promise.
5. **Follow the house format.** A proposal, quote or letter of a kind the vault holds follows the latest sent instance's structure, not its line breaks; name any section you would add.
6. **No reason, no draft.** With nothing in the vault saying why to write now, ask for the reason.
7. **In the sender's voice**, per [voice-profile.md](voice-profile.md#where-it-lives).
8. **Once it has gone out**, a draft saved in the vault is compared with the sent mail ([voice-profile.md](voice-profile.md#corrections)), then deleted ([operating-discipline.md](operating-discipline.md#deleting-a-file)).
