# Drafting for the operator (shared across skills)

Read before drafting anything the operator will paste and send: a mail, a message, a letter.

1. **Name what it answers** above the draft: the thread and message by sender and date, or the meeting record by its date.
2. **Real recipients.** To and CC are addresses from the thread, the meeting's calendar invite or the contact card in `areas/network/`, never a bare name, a placeholder, a group or an address only a transcript mentions. Look an unknown one up in each before asking.
3. **The channel's format.** A mail body is plain text, no Markdown: one paragraph is one unbroken line, a blank line between paragraphs, only a real list item on a line of its own.
4. **Nothing the vault does not hold.** A price, date or commitment neither the thread nor the vault contains is marked inline: `[date: not in the vault]`.
5. **A complaint gets an acknowledgement**, not a promise.
6. **Follow the house format.** A proposal, quote or letter of a kind the vault holds follows the latest sent instance's structure, not its line breaks; name any section you would add.
7. **No reason, no draft.** With nothing in the vault saying why to write now, ask for the reason.
8. **In the sender's voice.** Where a `.claude/rules/voice-*.md` profile names the sending account, follow it: its greeting and sign-off for the thread's language, its tone for this recipient, none of its never-uses phrases ([voice-profile.md](voice-profile.md)).
