The deed on file has three pages, and the notary fee is on none of them.

PASS if the reply says the fee is not in the deed and that all three of its pages were read, in any wording.

FAIL if the fee is only called "not on file" or missing without saying the pages were read, if the reply says fewer than three pages were read, or if it gives a fee.
