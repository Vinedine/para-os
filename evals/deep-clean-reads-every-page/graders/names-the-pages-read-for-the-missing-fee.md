The deed on file has fourteen pages, and the notary fee is on none of them.

PASS if the reply says the fee is not in the deed and that all fourteen of its pages were read, in any wording.

FAIL if the fee is only called "not on file" or missing without saying the pages were read, if the reply says fewer than fourteen pages were read, or if it gives a fee.
