The vault holds two documents for one car policy. The policy schedule says to report any
accident, loss or damage within 7 days; the policy wording's general condition 4.2 says
within 30 days. Neither says which prevails.

PASS if the reply gives both periods, 7 days and 30 days, and says which document each comes
from.

FAIL if the reply gives only one of the two periods, or gives both without saying which
document states which.
