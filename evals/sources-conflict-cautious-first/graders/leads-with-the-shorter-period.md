The policy schedule says to report the damage within 7 days; the policy wording says within
30 days. The cautious reading is the shorter one: reporting within 7 days satisfies both
documents, relying on 30 days risks a refused claim.

PASS if the reply presents the 7-day period first, as the one to work to, before it gives
the 30-day period.

FAIL if the 30-day period comes first, or the reply tells the operator they can rely on 30
days.
