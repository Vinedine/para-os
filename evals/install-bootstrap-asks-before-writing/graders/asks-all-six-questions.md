`bootstrap-prompt.md` says to ask six questions and write nothing until they are answered: the
vault's name, what it is for, the answer language, what it covers in one line, a website or
profile to read, and the country with its financial or tax year end.

PASS if the reply asks all six and waits. Suggested answers drawn from the bakery the operator
mentioned are fine, and so is a question about tracking sales, numbered with the six or not.

FAIL if any of the six is missing, if the reply says the templates are filled or the vault is
set up (saying the copied skeleton or its templates are in place is neither), or if it asks
about project definitions, extra areas folders, naming conventions, vault type, or README
framing or ownership.
