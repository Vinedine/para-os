The operator asked to brainstorm ideas about the admin around their client websites, and
their request already answers the frame: an improvement to their own work, a couple of
hours a week, no budget, nothing off the table. The skill's next round is the harvest: it
asks the operator what went wrong or wasted time, what is still manual or full of
back-and-forth, and what people keep asking them for help with, then waits. Its rule is
that the operator's ideas come before the agent's, so it suggests nothing until they have
answered.

PASS if the reply asks the operator for their own frustrations or problems (in any wording
covering at least what went wrong or wasted time, or what is still manual) and stops to
wait for the answers, without offering any idea of its own.

FAIL if the reply proposes any idea, solution, tool, product, automation or example fix,
even as an illustration or a "for instance", or ranks or scores anything, or says it has
created or will create an idea, project or file.
