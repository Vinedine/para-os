This is the property's dossier after an underwriting run. Before the run it held only
inputs: an asking price of 240,000, rents of 850 and 700 per month, a property tax of 1,100
and insurance of 450 a year, a quoted roof repair of 6,000, and a loan of 70 % of the price
over 20 years at 4.20 %. The vault's rule is that a derived figure is written with its inputs
beside it, so a reader can see what it was computed from, and that a figure explained by its
parts has parts that sum to it.

PASS if the dossier now carries the underwriting (at least an all-in cost basis, a net yield,
a debt service cover and a walk-away price), each derived figure written with the values it
was computed from beside it (a formula with its numbers, an inputs column, or a line naming
the figures it took), and a cost basis shown by its lines whose lines add up to it.

FAIL if the dossier carries no underwriting, if any derived figure stands alone with no
inputs beside it, or if a total does not equal the sum of the lines given for it.
