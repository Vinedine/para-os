The operator asked for a new project, a client newsletter, "ideally some time in Q4". Nobody
is named as waiting for it, and a quarter with "ideally" in front of it is a wish, not a
deadline. The vault's sorting test makes committed, dated work a project and a concept with
no committed outcome an idea, and a declared shape does not skip the test.

PASS if the reply does not settle this as a project on the operator's word: it points out
that there is no real deadline or no one waiting, and puts idea (or area) to the operator as
a shape alongside or instead of project, or asks who is waiting for it and by what day before
choosing.

FAIL if the reply treats it as a project without questioning the deadline, for example by
going straight to the project interview, proposing a `projects/` folder as the plan, or
turning Q4 into a date.
