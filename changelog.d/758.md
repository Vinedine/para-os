## Also new

- **The global brief is a dashboard.** One page across all your vaults: what is due today, the rest of the week and the next two weeks beside every calendar's meetings, your own vaults by area and by person, and every vault's health card. A vault you look after for someone else shows its health only, and a busy one of your own can be counted instead of listed.

## What to do

- If you run several vaults: to have a busy vault of your own counted rather than listed in the global brief, add `"brief": "summary"` to its entry in your vault registry.
