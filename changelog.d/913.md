## Changelog

- Unattended triage's switched-on actions live in the registry: `vaults.json`, a vault entry's `auto_triage`
- What a restore switched off lives in the cache: `cache/auto-triage/demoted.json`

## Fixes

- **The actions a scheduled triage may take now sit with the vault they belong to.** They are a vault's `auto_triage` list in `vaults.json`, so renaming or adding a vault can no longer leave them silently unmatched. A restore writes only to the cache; `switch` is the one command that edits the registry, after a backup.

## What to do

- After you upgrade: run `py -3 ~/.claude/skills/para-triage/scripts/auto_execute.py migrate` once. It moves `auto-triage.json` into the registry and the cache and renames it `auto-triage.json.migrated`; until then the older file keeps working.
