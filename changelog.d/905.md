## Also new

- **The vault registry says who pulls a vault.** A vault another machine pulls is left out of this machine's ingest and shows its health in the global brief with no triage count. A vault whose work is over is deleted from the registry rather than marked retired.

## What to do

- If you run several vaults: in your vault registry, add `"machine"` naming who pulls each vault another machine ingests, and delete the entry of any vault marked `"retired"`, since the audit and the global brief no longer skip it.
