## Also new

- **The vault registry says who pulls a vault and when one was retired.** A vault another machine pulls is left out of this machine's ingest and shows its health in the global brief with no triage count; a retired vault is listed as retired, never as unhealthy.

## What to do

- If you run several vaults: in your vault registry, add `"machine"` naming who pulls each vault another machine ingests, and replace `"retired": true` with the date the vault was retired.
