## Changelog

**`/para-prep` prepares a meeting from the vault's own files.** A new read-only base skill, `/para-prep [<event>|<person>|today]`, answering in chat and writing nothing. It reads the agenda as `/para-daily-brief` builds it, matches each attendee to an `areas/network/` card (an address the card carries, else the whole name in either word order, an alias, or the card's file name), and gathers per meeting: each card's `**Kind:**` and open items; the entities the card links, or whose brief links it back, with a staged entity's stage, days in stage, `Last touch` and `Signer`; the open items naming the person in any other action file; the newest record naming them in a live `sources/` or in `archive/meetings/`, and any still unfiled in `triage/`; and an entity's `**Do not raise:**` list. A staged entity's objective starts as its current stage's exit criterion for the operator to sharpen, and with no lifecycle it is a question. An attendee with no card is flagged and offered `/para-new`, and the prep closes on what the vault does not hold, as questions. The mechanics are `para-prep/scripts/prep_scan.py`; the shared library gains `contact_names`, which `/para-deep-clean` now reads from there. Reaction: copy `base/.claude/skills/para-prep/` from the clone, as a tree, to wherever the vault's other `/para-*` skills are installed, bundled or user-level; re-sync `para-shared/` and `/para-deep-clean`; and name `/para-prep` wherever the vault's `CLAUDE.md` lists its skills.

## What changes for you

A new skill, `/para-prep`, gets you ready for a meeting from what your vault already holds: who is coming, what is open with each of them in both directions, what happened last time and what you promised, and what the vault does not know yet, as questions to ask. It reads only, and never changes a file.
