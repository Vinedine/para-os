## Changelog

**A vault chooses what its contact cards may hold.** The `### Where a checkbox may live` table gains an `areas/network/` row at one of three levels: `yes` (any action about the person, the default and today's behaviour), `relationship only` (replies, introductions, thanks and check-ins; the rest goes to the entity it serves) or `never` (no checkbox; `## Next actions` carries the empty sentinel, optionally one line pointing at the live work). The contacts bullet, "Which file an action goes in", the go/no-go sentence and the archive-routing sentence defer to the row, and `## Who writes this vault` no longer overrides them: with no row, a roster implies `never`. A card may carry an optional `## History` of closed items before `## Next actions`. `paraos_vault.misplaced_checkboxes` reports a card's open and ticked checkboxes at `never`, so `/para-daily-brief` flags the open ones and `/para-deep-clean` Phase 1 repairs both; `/para-archive` offers a contact file only as the row allows; `/para-upgrade`'s new `checkboxes` block names any table row the vault lacks. Reaction: re-sync every `para-*` skill and `para-shared`. Add the `areas/network/` row as the scan's `checkboxes.missing` writes it, at `checkboxes.contact_card_level` (`never` for a vault with `## Who writes this vault`, else `yes`), and replace the clauses the template rewrote (the contacts bullet, the per-contact line in "Which file an action goes in", the go/no-go and archive-routing sentences) with base's, keeping the vault's own wording elsewhere. Never change a level the vault already declares. In a vault at `never`, demote each ticked card item to a plain bullet in the card's `## History` section (checkbox and `✅` stripped, `_(closed YYYY-MM-DD)_` appended), and route each open one to the project or area it serves, or drop it, one approval per item.

## What changes for you

You can now decide whether people's contact cards hold to-dos: all of them as today, only relationship ones such as a reply or a thank-you, or none, with every action living in its project or area. The upgrade adds the setting at what your vault does today and asks before moving any item.
