## Changelog

**A vault carries no unit tests.** `INSTALL.md` copies `base/` without its `test_*.py`, which stay in the kit beside the scripts they cover; `/para-upgrade` compares a skill copy to its master with those files left out on both sides, so one the master has is never `missing` and a leftover one is never `extra`, and it verifies a synced skill script by re-running the scan rather than by running a suite. Reaction: delete every `test_*.py` under the vault's `.claude/skills/para-*/scripts/` and `.claude/skills/para-shared/scripts/`, and under `~/.claude/skills/` where the skills live there, one approval for the batch.

## What changes for you

Your vault no longer carries the kit's own test files, which were half of what the install copied in and did nothing for you.

## Do you need to do anything?

Run `/para-upgrade`. It proposes deleting the test files your vault already has.
