## Changelog

**`base/` is exactly what a vault receives.** The kit's unit suites move from each skill's `scripts/` folder to `tools/tests/`, one `test_<script>.py` per script, so `INSTALL.md` copies `base/` whole with nothing to leave out; `/para-upgrade` compares a skill copy to its master with any `test_*.py` left out on both sides, so one an older master ships is never `missing` and a leftover one is never `extra`, and it verifies a synced skill script by re-running the scan rather than by running a suite beside it. Reaction: delete every `test_*.py` under the vault's `.claude/skills/para-*/scripts/` and `.claude/skills/para-shared/scripts/`, and under `~/.claude/skills/` where the skills live there, one approval for the batch.

## What changes for you

Your vault no longer carries the kit's own test files, which were half of what the install copied in and did nothing for you.

## Do you need to do anything?

Run `/para-upgrade`. It proposes deleting the test files your vault already has.
