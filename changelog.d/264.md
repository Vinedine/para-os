## Changelog

**A vault declares its locale.** `## Language` gains a `**Locale:**` paragraph: country, currency as an ISO 4217 code with the symbol the vault writes for it, financial year end, the number convention the vault writes in, date order in prose and time zone as an IANA name, the line being the one home of the six, which `README.md`'s Identity links rather than restates; `paraos_vault.locale` reads it. `.claude/rules/figures.md` gains `## Amounts`: an amount carries the declared currency's code or symbol and any other currency its own code, a new one is written in the declared convention and an existing one is never rewritten, an amount whose separators (or a date whose order) are ambiguous is read by the declaration and confirmed against its source where a wrong reading would change a decision, and a quarter or year is the declared financial year's. `/para-pipeline`'s quarter is a quarter of the declared financial year, the calendar's where none is declared, and the scan reports `quarter_start`, `quarter_end` and `year_end_unread`. `para-shared/timestamps.md` converts a time shown or written from its own offset into the declared zone, never reads its digits, and reports an offset that contradicts its zone label; `/para-daily-brief`'s agenda and `connectors.md` link it. ## What changes for you

Your vault now states its country, currency, financial year, number and date format and time zone in one line. Amounts carry their currency, an ambiguous figure is read the way your vault writes them, the pipeline's quarter follows your financial year, and meeting times are shown in your time zone. The upgrade fills the line from what your vault already says and asks you only for the rest.
