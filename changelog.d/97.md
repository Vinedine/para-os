## Changelog

**Integrations.** New: `plaud` at 2026.10.01, the counterpart of `pocket` for the [Plaud](https://www.plaud.ai/) wearable recorders. `plaud.py login` signs in once through the browser (OAuth with PKCE as the public client of Plaud's own CLI, on a loopback redirect at `localhost:8199`) and keeps refreshing tokens in `secrets/plaud.json`; a refused refresh asks for `login` again. It reads Plaud's third-party API with GET only, writes one note per recording (AI summary and a timestamped transcript, dated when the recording started, in local time) into `triage/`, and routes across sibling vaults by title prefix under pocket's and granola's rule, a vault that is the only destination needing no `plaud.config.json` at all. A recording missing its transcript or summary is held and retried next run until 24 hours after Plaud received it, then written with what exists; one whose transcript cannot be parsed is held with the `--dump` command that shows it. Its dedup ledger is `data/plaud/synced.json`. Unchanged: `granola`, `outlook` and `pocket`. Reaction: none for an existing vault; a vault adopting it copies `plaud.py` per `integrations/plaud/README.md`.

## What changes for you

If you record on a Plaud NotePin or Note, its summaries and transcripts can now land in your vault's triage folder the way Pocket's and Granola's do, after a one-time sign-in in your browser.
