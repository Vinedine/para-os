## Changelog

**`/para-archive` closes out what it archives.** A new Step 9, in `references/close-out.md`, runs once the verify call is clean: it proposes one line for the root `README.md`'s `## Track record` linking the archived brief, or the extension of a line already there, with figures only as `figures.md` allows; it routes at most three lessons to a playbook in `resources/`, a `CLAUDE.md` rule or a working preference, and reports any lesson left in the closed brief as never read again; and for a won deal it records the outcome in the client's own words, verbatim, linked to its source file, with a clearance of `named`, `anonymised` or `internal only`. The plan call's `lifecycle` block gains `won`. Reaction: re-sync `/para-archive`.

## What changes for you

Archiving a finished project now offers a line for your track record, asks what you would do differently next time and files the answer where you will read it again, and for a won client keeps their own words about the result. You can skip any of the three.
