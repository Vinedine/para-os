## Changelog

**A voice profile per sender, so a draft in their name reads as theirs.** New `para-shared/voice-profile.md`: a vault may keep one profile per person who sends from it, `.claude/rules/voice-<firstname-lastname>.md`, whose `paths:` names its own path so it loads only when read, whose `**Accounts:**` line names the accounts it covers, and which `CLAUDE.md` points at in a paragraph of its own under `## Memory`, in the convention form. It holds fixed traits per language (greeting, sign-off, sentence length, contractions, where the ask lands, what they call their own service), tone rows keyed on what the vault records about a recipient (a card's `**Kind:**`, an entity's stage, the thread's language), a never-uses list and one verbatim sample with every other person replaced by a role; every line ends in `inferred (n/N samples)` or `operator (date, the correction quoted)`, a trait needs three samples and a never-uses entry is absent from all of them. It is built only on its person's ask, from their own words in the last 12 months of sent mail (read per `connectors.md`) or mails they hand over, with quoted history, forwards, the auto-signature and legal footers stripped; with no samples it asks for three and stops. A rebuild replaces inferred lines only. A correction to a draft stating a standing trait is proposed as a profile line the first time, not the second; a one-off tone change is not. `drafting.md` gains step 8, reading the profile that names the sending account; the template's `## Memory` paragraph names the profile; `working-preferences.md` sends how someone writes to it; `/para-audit` no longer reports a `voice-*.md` rule file as a topic file. Reaction: re-sync `para-shared` (and `/para-audit` where installed); bring the `## Memory` paragraph to base's wording and add base's last sentence to the **Out:** bullet of the vault's `.claude/rules/working-preferences.md`, keeping its preferences. No profile is created by the upgrade: a vault gains one only when its person asks.

## What changes for you

You can ask the assistant to learn how you write from your sent mail, and its drafts then open, close and phrase things the way you do. Every line it learns shows its evidence, and a correction you make to a draft becomes a line only when you say yes.
