## Changelog

**After a meeting record is filed, `/para-triage` proposes the entity's updates and a follow-up draft.** A dated conversation record filed for an entity whose shape declares `Last touch` carries a follow-on set, approved with its item (`para-triage/references/after-a-meeting.md`): Last touch set to the record's own date plus one clause, only if newer, shown as current then proposed; Signer where the record names who commits and the shape declares the field; one next step, our earliest commitment, on the contact's card as far as the `areas/network/` row allows and otherwise in the entity's own home, and their earliest commitment as one `Waiting on` line homed the same way; a stage question only where the current stage's exit criterion was met, never a Stage edit; and a follow-up draft, never sent. On the table path, follow-on edits and drafts go under the table in full, never by reference to an earlier message. `para-shared/drafting.md` adds the meeting's calendar invite as a recipient source and refuses an address said or written in a meeting's notes or transcript. `triage_scan.py` reports the vault's `contact_card_level`. The sales module's `deal-brief.md` no longer says Last touch is rewritten by hand. Reaction: re-sync `/para-triage` and `para-shared`; a vault carrying the sales module re-copies `.claude/rules/deal-brief.md`.

## What changes for you

When triage files the notes of a meeting with a prospect or another tracked contact, it now proposes the updates you used to make by hand: the date of last contact, who signs, the one next step, and a follow-up mail to send. It asks whether the deal moved on rather than moving it.
