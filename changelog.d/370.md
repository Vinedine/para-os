## Changelog

**`para-shared/` says the same rules in fewer words.** The twelve shared files drop from about 9,400 words to 5,400: rationale sentences go, rules said twice merge, and the platform detail a current model carries on its own is cut. `test-run.md` counts four finding classes (not followable, contradiction, a wrong result caught, a script failure); a step that had to be guessed at or cost a round trip is a rule to shorten, not a finding. Every heading another skill links to stays. Reaction: re-sync `para-shared/`.
