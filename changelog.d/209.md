## Changelog

**Actions close when the work is done, and stay short.** A file holds at most 8 open items, replacing the 12-item grooming warning: at 8, adding one means closing or demoting one first. An action's headline (its bold lead, or the whole line without one) stays under 120 characters, and an item that moves is closed and its successor written as a new line. Every tick writes `✅ <date>`; an operator's "sent", "paid" or "done" closes the matching item at once; a ticked `🔁` item is rolled forward by `paraos_vault.py next-occurrence`; when work on an entity ends, `paraos_vault.py open-items` lists what to reconcile in one question; a follow-up is offered as a checkbox, never left as prose (`para-shared/operating-discipline.md`, `## Closing and adding actions`). A folder whose work is tracked elsewhere gets its own `never` row in the checkbox table, and every skill routes around it. `/para-daily-brief` flags a file over the cap, a headline over 120 characters and open checkboxes outside the action files; `/para-deep-clean` offers one batched demotion per file of the undated items untouched for 30 days.
