- In a vault whose `## Triage sources` says who pulls a row other than as `pulled by <name>` with a name its `## Who writes this vault` roster holds, ask the operator who pulls it, then rewrite it in that form, or drop it in a vault with no roster: `CLAUDE.md`

## Fixes

- **A mailbox is no longer pulled when the vault cannot confirm who pulls it.** Where a triage source names who pulls it but the vault cannot tell who that is (no list of who writes the vault, a name not on it, no record of who you are on this computer, or other wording than "pulled by" and a name), triage and the cross-vault ingest leave it alone and name it in their report. The ingest also refuses a routing reason that names a vault the item is not sent to.
