## Changelog

**A property figure worked out from others is computed in code, with its inputs beside it.** `property-dossier.md` gains `## Derived figures`: a total, yield, cover ratio, margin, per-unit amount or bid level is computed by running code, never by mental arithmetic, and written with the values it took beside it; a figure explained by its parts has parts that sum to it; a deal sheet derives none. `/property-underwrite` Step 5 computes every derived figure, the scenarios, bid zone and walk-away included, in a Python snippet it runs, each input named with its source and each total asserted against its parts. `/property-dealsheet`'s readiness check stops on a figure the sheet prints that the dossier does not state, and on a total whose parts do not sum to it, as a `/property-underwrite` gap; it no longer adds a divided building's costs into the cost basis itself. `/property-reconcile`'s cascade recomputes from the inputs recorded beside each figure. Reaction: none for a vault whose own entities are not properties it buys, renovates, sells or holds. A real-estate vault copies `property-dossier.md` and re-syncs `property-underwrite`, `property-dealsheet` and `property-reconcile`. Existing dossier figures stay as written and are never recomputed by the upgrade.

## What changes for you

In a property vault, every number the underwriting works out (the all-in cost, the yields, the loan cover, the margins, the bid and the walk-away price) is now calculated by a small program rather than in the assistant's head, and the property's document shows what each one was calculated from. The deal sheet no longer fills in a missing total or margin on its own: it stops and asks for the underwriting first.

## Do you need to do anything?

Only in a property vault: a deal sheet for a property underwritten before this change may stop on a total or margin its document never stated. Run `/property-underwrite` on that property, then build the sheet again.
