## Changelog

**`/para-triage` can read the operator's sent mail, per mailbox, for promises made and messages left unanswered.** A `## Triage sources` mailbox row may add `📤 sent` to its `Type`, which `paraos_vault.triage_sources` reads as `sent`; `para-shared/connectors.md` gains the sent pass in its candidate table (Gmail `in:sent newer_than:14d`, `outlook.py fetch --sent`) on the same seen-ledger and watermark, run by `/para-triage` alone, even where `/para-ingest` covered the inbox (the scan's `plan` `sent`). Triage reads every message the operator wrote in a thread for a dated promise no later message shows kept, and proposes it as **Update existing** or **Add action** with `📅` on the promised date, a weekday computed by `paraos_vault.py weekday-after`. A thread the operator wrote last, asking something, becomes a `Waiting on` line (`since` its sent date) or **Update existing** once the scan's `threads[].unanswered` counts 5 working days, Monday to Friday with public holidays counted as working days; before that it is neither asked nor ledgered. Reaction: re-sync `para-shared/` and `/para-triage`. Add `📤 sent` to a row only on the operator's say, never unasked, and never to a mailbox that copies its sent mail into its inbox.

**Integrations.** Updated to 2026.10.01: `outlook` (`fetch --sent` also reads Sent Items, Graph's `sentitems`, over at least 14 days, so each thread carries the owner's own messages; GET only, and off without the flag). Reaction: straight file copy of `outlook.py` where installed.

## What changes for you

Triage can now look through the mail you sent, not only what you received, on a mailbox you switch it on for. When you promised something by a day ("price to you Wednesday"), it offers a to-do dated that day, and when you sent a proposal or a question that has had no answer for a working week, it offers to note that you are waiting on that person.

## Do you need to do anything?

To switch it on for a mailbox, add `📤 sent` after the type in that mailbox's row of your vault's triage sources table. Leave it off for a shared mailbox that already copies sent mail into its inbox.
